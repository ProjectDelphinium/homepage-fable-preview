Three crises, one cause: disengagement. Doing nothing is the most expensive option you have.

Canvas knows who's falling behind. It just won't tell you.

Every missed login, late assignment, and skipped module is a warning sign. Canvas records all the signals, then scatters them across pages nobody has time to read. By the time a struggling student shows up in the gradebook, the window to help them has often already closed.

  • Invisible until it's too late

    Struggling students rarely speak up. They go quiet, one missed assignment at a time, until they're too far behind to catch up.

  • Thirty messages, one at a time

    Every check-in is written by hand, one student at a time. So most of the messages that could save a student never get sent.

  • Detective work, not teaching

    You get hours of digging through gradebooks and reports, paid for with nights and weekends instead of time with students.

  • Parents in the dark

    Parents want to be the coach in their kid's corner, but most find out they're struggling after it's too late.

Stop hunting for struggling students, Delphinium brings them to you.

Delphinium is built on three proven theories of student engagement

  • Self-regulated learning

    When students have the right information, they can set meaningful goals, monitor their progress, and reflect on what's working, adapting and driving their own learning.

    In Delphinium

    • Students see their progress, performance, and priorities at a glance
    • Progress updates create a feedback loop students use to adjust and improve
    • A clear view of the whole journey helps students set goals and take ownership

  • Behavioral economics (Nudge theory)

    When next steps are clear, timely, and easy to act on, students are more likely to follow through and build momentum toward success.

    In Delphinium

    • Clear visual cues make priorities and next steps easy to follow
    • Timely nudges prompt action before students fall behind
    • Celebrations and success signals reinforce momentum and persistence

  • Social presence

    When students feel seen, connected, and supported, they are more likely to engage, participate, and persist.

    In Delphinium

    • Personalized outreach helps students feel seen and valued
    • Students feel the class moving around them and know they're not alone
    • Teachers reach the right student at the right moment, before silence turns into withdrawal
