## Personas (ranked)
1. Primary decision: Director of Digital / Online Learning / Virtual Programs (K-12 online, virtual academy, cyber charter, district VA)
2. Influence + often co-buy: Principal / Head of virtual school
3. Gate / IT: CTO / IT Director / Canvas admin (LTI, no rip-and-replace, low risk)
4. Soft influence: teachers / facilitators (feel the pain; rarely buy)
5. Secondary ICP: higher-ed Canvas — HE must not own the homepage

Lands via Google / LinkedIn / conference → need proof fast.
Decides: DOL + principal (+ sometimes CTO).

## One job of the homepage
Book a conversation / demo after making one thing obvious: Delphinium works with Canvas to turn engagement into action, with a measured outcome.
Primary CTA → schedule (https://delphi-me.com/schedule-jared).
Proof (31% / 72%) supports the CTA — not the only job.
Not a K12-vs-HE chooser. K-12 online = default story; HE = secondary path.

## Approved public language
USE (do not strengthen):
- Canvas LMS enhancement / works with Canvas. Canvas delivers and organizes learning; Delphinium helps schools understand engagement and turn Canvas data into action.
- Proof: 72 fully online classes, 6,000+ students, same courses/teachers/content → course failure dropped 31%. 72% of students more/much more motivating.
- Actions: identify → understand → communicate → intervene with support → engage.
- Post-ESSER: maximize the Canvas you already own.
- Named social proof when SOURCE names them: Natalie Niederhauser; Tiffany Dance. Prefer name + role; role-only if name not approved.
- Official video: https://www.youtube.com/@DelphiniumEngage

AVOID on public site:
- “Delphinium changes what the student does” (too strong publicly)
- Unnamed competitor comparisons
- “Up to 31%” (it is 31%)
- Attacking Canvas
- Invented dollars, security/compliance, new customers, stronger outcomes
- “Teachers always” / “parents typically” without evidence

## Messaging already in market (echo)
- Human problem first: teachers chase students who haven’t started; parents learn late when the grade has taken a hit
- Motivation = buying question for online programs
- Texas virtual-academy boom = engagement at Day 180, not launch day
- New tech / old model: more reports ≠ student work; turn information into action
- Story: Hook → Problem → Insight → Bridge → Delphinium → Proof → Takeaway → CTA
- CTAs land on delphi-me.com

## Soft / hard no
Hard: fabricate proof; peer-reviewed claim; Michigan retention; student data; LMS feature war; rip-and-replace Canvas; unapproved auto-publish; invent security badges.
Soft: HE as homepage hero; deep engineering; doom-only openers; public battlecards; philosophy without outcomes.
