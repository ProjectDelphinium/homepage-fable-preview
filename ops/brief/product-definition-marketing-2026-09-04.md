# What Delphinium IS (Marketing, 2026-09-04)
Canonical public: SOURCE.md + Sey rules.

## IS
Canvas LMS enhancement that helps schools understand engagement and turn Canvas data into action — identify who’s stuck, understand why, communicate with students and families, intervene with support, and give students a reason to stay engaged.

Shorter: works with Canvas to turn engagement into action — with a measured outcome.

## IS NOT
LMS / Canvas replacement / rip-and-replace / a website / standalone student app replacing the course / admin analytics alone / competitor takedown / public “changes what the student does.”

## Friend-tell peak
“Delphinium is a layer on Canvas that helps students stay engaged — and in 72 fully online classes with the same courses and teachers, course failure dropped 31%.”
