Why it exists

Forty-one students haven't started this week. The gradebook still looks fine.

In an online school, silence doesn't look like anything. It looks like blanks in a published Canvas course. Then someone opens a spreadsheet and starts writing emails.

Every week, someone starts chasing.

Teachers chase. Counselors chase. A principal pulls a report to find out who to chase. And the people who could help most, the parents, usually hear about it last.

"When a parent finds out late, the grade has already taken a hit."

  • Mon Export "missing assignments" report. Sort. Copy names into an email.
  • Tue Twelve individual emails before first period. Three bounce.
  • Wed Counselor asks which kids to call. Re-run the report.
  • Thu Student logs in, sees a gray list of 40 links, logs out.
  • Week 7 Parent calls: "Why is my kid failing? Nobody told me."

More reports are not more student work. Engagement has to turn into action.

Another dashboard can tell you who is slipping. It can't give a fifteen-year-old a reason to open the course, and it can't message the kid and the family for you at 7:40 on a Monday.

That's the part nobody built for. The part after the report.

Canvas delivers content. Delphinium delivers engagement.

Delphinium is an engagement layer that lives inside the Canvas you already run. It turns an existing course into a visual, motivating experience for students and an early-warning system for teachers, in about three minutes. No migration. Teachers keep teaching in Canvas. You just turn it on.

Families in the loop

Parents shouldn't need Canvas literacy to see how their kid is doing.

Parents and observers get the same color-coded picture the student and teacher see, live, in the Canvas parent app. When a student needs support, families are on the message from the start, calmly, early, before the grade takes the hit.

  • Color-coded parent and observer viewGreen, yellow, red. Same live picture as the student. No decoding required.
  • Community BuilderWeekly progress to families, auto-translated into their preferred language. 160+ languages.
  • Celebrate wins with parents, not only rescuesPositive notes go home too. Transparency lets parents coach, and the parent-teacher relationship stays constructive.
  • Include families on outreachInclude observers, only observers, or CC a counselor on any send from Control Tower.
