Delphinium turns data into action

Canvas is data-rich, but insight-poor.

Canvas captures a lot: assignments, gradebook, modules, analytics. But it lives on pages built for storage, not engagement.

  • The student sees

    a gray wall of text. The signals they need, like progress and what to do next, are buried. A struggling student can fade with nothing to pull them back.

  • The parent wants

    to be the cheerleader and coach their kid needs, but gets data without a story they can act on.

  • The teacher faces

    hours of slow manual work to find and reach at-risk students, one at a time. Paid for in lost evenings.

What you need is a way to turn that data into action: Delphinium.

What your school gets

Delphinium Core · Control Tower

Know where every student stands, and act on it without leaving the page.

Turn Delphinium on and your existing courses become an early-warning system, so teachers can step in before a slipping grade becomes a failed course.

Engagement Builder

Turn the gray wall of text into a class students want to open.

  • Nudges, celebrations, and progress trackers that reward momentum

Community Builder · Message Center

Reach the right student at the right moment, without the manual email.

Stop blasting the whole class. Target exactly the students who need to hear from you.

  • Templates and relative-date schedules that follow each teacher across sections and semesters
  • Rules like “only send if points are below 50” reach at-risk students automatically
  • One message lands as a Canvas message, announcement, to-do item, banner, and email
  • Celebrate students who are thriving, not just rescue the ones who aren't
~12emails a day → ~6messages a week

Families stay in the loop. Parents and observers get the same color-coded picture teachers see, readable at a glance with no Canvas know-how required. Include them on messages, share the good news, and auto-translate so every family hears from you in their own language.
