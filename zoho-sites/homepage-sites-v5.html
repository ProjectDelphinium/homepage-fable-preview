Delphinium Core · Control Tower

Turn Canvas into an early-warning system.

Canvas is data-rich, but insight-poor. Control Tower turns that data into action, so teachers know where every student stands and can step in before a slipping grade becomes a failed course.

  • Every student red, yellow, or green from live Canvas data
  • Click any student for grades, progress, engagement, and who is observing
  • Filter to struggling students and message all of them, families included, in about 40 seconds
  • Runs on the Canvas courses you already have, including in the Canvas parent app
