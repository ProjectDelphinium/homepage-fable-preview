## Your Privacy is Important to Us

At Delphi M.E., we are committed to respecting your privacy. We recognize that when you choose to provide us with information about yourself, you trust us to act in a responsible manner and to protect and safely manage any Personal Information that you share with us.

"Personal Information" means any information that may be used to identify an individual, including, but not limited to, a first and last name, a physical address and an email address or other contact information, whether at work or at home.

Please read our privacy policy carefully to get a clear understanding of how we collect, use, protect or otherwise handle your Personally Identifiable Information. When reading through this Privacy Policy, please read the entire contents and do not rely on headings, which are illustrative of some but not all the contents and should not be read so as to limit any of the terms and conditions detailed below each heading.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with the Delphinium End User License Agreement at https://www.delphi-me.com/eula.

- "You" and "Your" refers jointly and severally to the individual accessing this site and the Delphi M.E. customer or partner by which You are employed or with which You are related.
- "Materials" refers to the software, courseware, toolkits, documentation and all other information, whether tangible or intangible, that Delphi M.E. makes available.

## Information We Collect

- From the learning management system (Canvas / LTI) when an institution enables Delphinium in a course, we may receive:
  - LTI / OAuth access and identity tokens needed to authenticate the session;
  - Course context (course ID, title, section identifiers);
  - User roster fields provided by the LMS for authorized educational use (for example: name, email, LMS user ID, role such as learner/instructor);
  - Activity and gamification data generated in Delphinium (progress, points, badges, engagement events tied to the course);
  - Configuration set by instructors/admins.

  Exact fields depend on the institution's LMS settings and the LTI version in use.
- From institutions / Authorized Users: admin contacts, billing contacts, support tickets, training registrations.
- From parents/guardians (opt-in SMS): mobile numbers and SMS consent records (see SMS/Text Message Data Privacy below).
- From websites: device identifiers as described below; contact-form submissions.

We do not require students to create a separate Delphinium consumer account outside the institution's LMS path.

## When We Collect Information

We collect information primarily when the institution installs/configures Delphinium and when users launch the LTI tool; also when parents opt in to SMS; when Authorized Users contact support or use marketing/docs sites.

## How We Use Information

We use information solely to provide, secure, support, and improve the contracted educational Services for the Purchaser; to send Authorized User / parent communications they have consented to or that the Purchaser authorized under the Purchase Agreement; and for de-identified/aggregate analytics. We do not use student Personal Information for targeted advertising or for sale.

## How We Protect Your Information

We implement a variety of security measures when a user enters, submits, or accesses their information to maintain the safety of your personal information. Your personal information is contained behind secured networks and is only accessible by a limited number of persons who have special access rights to such systems, and are required to keep the information confidential. In addition, all sensitive information you supply is encrypted via Secure Socket Layer (SSL) technology. Also, Personal Information is stored in an encrypted format.

(See Retention and Deletion below for storage periods.)

## JWT and Similar Technologies

We do not use 'cookies'. We use JWT and similar technologies:

- Essential: required for login/session continuity and security of Delphinium and our sites.
- Functional: remember UI preferences between page loads.

We do not use advertising cookies on student LTI surfaces.

## How We Share Information

We do not sell Personal Information, and we do not use student Personal Information for targeted advertising.

We may disclose Personal Information only:

- To service providers / subprocessors that process data on our behalf to operate the Services (for example: cloud hosting, SMS message delivery, email delivery, customer support tooling), under contracts that limit use to our instructions and require appropriate confidentiality and security;
- If required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security;
- In a corporate transaction (merger, acquisition, asset sale), subject to equivalent protections and notice where required.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

A current list of material subprocessors is available upon request to support@delphi-me.com.

## Fair Information Practices

The Fair Information Practices Principles form the backbone of privacy law in the United States and the concepts they include have played a significant role in the development of data protection laws around the globe. Understanding the Fair Information Practice Principles and how they should be implemented is critical to comply with the various privacy laws that protect personal information.

### Security Incident Notice

If we become aware of a security incident that compromises Personal Information we process for a Purchaser, we will notify the Purchaser without unreasonable delay and in accordance with applicable law and any signed DPA after confirming the incident, via the security/admin contacts on file (email). We will provide information reasonably available about the nature of the incident, data involved, and remediation steps. Individual notice may be provided by or through the institution where required.

We also agree to the Individual Redress Principle which requires that individuals have the right to legally pursue enforceable rights against data collectors and processors who fail to adhere to the law. This principle requires not only that individuals have enforceable rights against data users, but also that individuals have recourse to courts or government agencies to investigate and/or prosecute non-compliance by data processors.

## CAN-SPAM Act

The CAN-SPAM Act is a law that sets the rules for commercial email, establishes requirements for commercial messages, gives recipients the right to have emails stopped from being sent to them, and spells out tough penalties for violations.

To be in accordance with CAN-SPAM, we agree to not collect email addresses for the purposes of commercial email through the Delphinium software.

## Retention and Deletion

We retain Personal Information only as long as needed to provide the contracted educational Services to the Purchaser institution, to comply with legal obligations, to resolve disputes, and to enforce our agreements.

- Live service data associated with an active institutional subscription: retained for the subscription term.
- After contract end or written deletion request from the Purchaser: we delete or de-identify Personal Information from production systems at the request of the client, except where retention is required by law or a signed DPA.
- Backups: residual copies in encrypted backups are overwritten on a backup rotation cycle.
- User Content you submit via a Website (non-education-record feedback/content): destroyed within 60 days of a verified request by You.

We do not retain Personal Information indefinitely. De-identified or aggregate data that cannot reasonably identify an individual may be retained for product improvement and analytics.

## SMS/Text Message Data Privacy

When parents or guardians opt in to receive SMS text messages through our platform, we collect their mobile phone number solely for the purpose of delivering text message notifications about their student's academic progress on behalf of the school or district. Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent data will not be shared with, sold to, or disclosed to third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. We may share mobile phone numbers with service providers who assist in delivering messages (such as our SMS gateway provider), but only as necessary to operate the service and subject to confidentiality obligations. Parents and guardians may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. Upon opt-out, their mobile number will be removed from active messaging lists. For questions about SMS messaging, reply HELP to any message or contact support@delphi-me.com.

## Children's Privacy (COPPA) and Student Users

Delphinium is offered to educational institutions, not as a consumer social product directed to children. When we receive Personal Information about students, including students under 13, we do so as a service provider to the school/district under the institution's direction and contract.

- School consent model: We rely on the educational institution to authorize collection and use of student information for the contracted educational purpose, consistent with applicable law (including COPPA where the school acts as agent for the parent).
- We do not condition a student's participation on disclosing more Personal Information than needed for the educational Service.
- We do not use student Personal Information for targeted advertising.
- Parents/guardians who want to review or delete student information should generally contact their school; we will support the school's verified request.
- This Privacy Policy is not a substitute for any COPPA direct notice the school may be required to provide.
